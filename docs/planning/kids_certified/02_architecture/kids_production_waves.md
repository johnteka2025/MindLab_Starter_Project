# Kids production waves

## Wave 1 - Pilot slice
Recommended categories: KC1, KC2, KC4, KC5
Recommended stages: K1 and K2
Purpose: prove the certification workflow with a reviewable, lower-stage batch.

## Wave 2 - Baseline coverage
Target: all 8 categories across K1-K3
Purpose: remove narrow-scope risk and support cross-category balance review.

## Wave 3 - Deepening
Target: all 8 categories across K4-K5
Purpose: extend only after lower-stage stability is proven.

## Wave 4 - Stabilization
Target: balance, analytics, QA evidence, release proof
Purpose: convert content into a release-ready Kids lane.
