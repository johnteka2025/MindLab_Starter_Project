# Kids category purpose matrix

## KC1 - Visual Matching & Discrimination
Purpose paragraph:
- Build early visual discrimination through matching shapes colors symbols and small scene details so players learn sameness difference grouping and confident scanning.

## KC2 - Patterns & Sequencing
Purpose paragraph:
- Develop rule detection through repeating changing and ordered sequences so players can predict what comes next and explain why the pattern works.

## KC3 - Memory & Recall
Purpose paragraph:
- Strengthen short-term memory and recall by asking players to retain recognize and retrieve visual or verbal information across short delays and repeated turns.

## KC4 - Spatial Reasoning
Purpose paragraph:
- Grow spatial understanding through position direction movement and transformation tasks so players can reason about where things are and how layouts change.

## KC5 - Numbers & Quantities
Purpose paragraph:
- Build number sense through counting comparing grouping and simple quantity logic so players connect symbols sets and amount relationships accurately.

## KC6 - Language & Word Thinking
Purpose paragraph:
- Expand language thinking through letters sounds words categories and verbal clues so players connect vocabulary patterns and meaning in age-appropriate ways.

## KC7 - Logic & Problem Solving
Purpose paragraph:
- Develop structured reasoning through clues elimination and simple strategy so players learn to test choices justify decisions and solve fair logic tasks.

## KC8 - Attention, Speed & Accuracy
Purpose paragraph:
- Improve focus pace and precision by training players to scan carefully spot differences sustain attention and balance speed with correct responses.
